A FEW GENTLE ANSWERS

Before you reach out.

Can I personalise a gift? +

Yes. The Bespoke Collection is designed around your wishes. Share who the gift is for and what you would like it to communicate, and we can discuss suitable personal touches.

Can I add grief counselling to any box? +

Grief support is an optional add-on. Contact us before ordering so we can confirm current availability, provider details, session format, and pricing.

Can you send a gift directly to someone? +

Delivery options and gift-note arrangements can be discussed when you enquire. We will confirm the available options before your order is finalised.

I’m not sure what to say. Can you help? +

You do not need to find perfect words. Tell us a little about the person and the moment, only what you feel comfortable sharing, and we can help you choose a thoughtful direction.
